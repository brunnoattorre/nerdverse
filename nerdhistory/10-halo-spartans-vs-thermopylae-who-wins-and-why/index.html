10 Halo Spartans vs Thermopylae: Who Wins and Why

We drop Master Chief and nine Spartan-IIs into the Hot Gates in 480 BCE and test whether anything the Persians have can hurt them.

Ten Spartan-IIs hold the pass and all go home; overall tier A.

Every NerdHistory fight

The Arrow Storm

S

The Spartans10 Spartan-IIs vs Xerxes' vanguard

A bow's arrow carries on the order of 100 joules, about a bowling ball dropped on your foot from four or five feet. The energy shield on MJOLNIR Mark V armor is built for far more than that. The shafts land like hail on a tin roof and the shields only flicker blue. Herodotus has the Spartan Dienekes joke that if the arrows blot out the sun, they'll fight in the shade. These ten don't even need the shade.

When the arrows fail, the infantry charges, and the pass funnels the spearmen together. Each assault rifle round carries dozens of times the energy of a Persian arrow, and wicker shields were never built for that. The front rank drops, the second trips over it, and the third climbs the pile. The bodies turn into a ramp. That is the Persians' best moment, and it comes from math, not tactics. Grace and Will cut down every man who crests it.

Nobody reaches the line. Soldiers stall at the sight of the pile, the men behind bump into them, and officers whip them forward, which is straight out of Herodotus. Their spears don't reach and their arrows bounce. Kelly-087 then runs up the cliff base to stop skirmishers going around the choke point. Her recorded top speed in Mark V armor is about 38 mph, and Halo canon calls her the fastest Spartan. The skirmishers never see her.

We score it S because nobody is hurt, scared or close to losing a shield. The only cost is ammo. Fred has six magazines, Linda four for her sniper rifle, Will five and Grace seven. John carries rifle ammo plus the squad's only rocket launcher, with both rockets untouched.

The Immortals

A

The Spartans10 Spartan-IIs vs Xerxes' 10,000 Immortals

The Immortals are Xerxes' professional core. They're named because their number never drops: lose a man and another steps in. Herodotus says their iron scale armor looked like fish scales. The armor and wicker shields were built to stop arrows. A rifle round goes through shield, scales and man, and sometimes the man behind him. They're not hard to kill, only hard to stop, and the ammo counter goes amber, then red.

Once the rifles run dry, the fight turns to spears and knives, and the width of the pass decides it. Herodotus puts the Hot Gates at half a plethron, about 50 feet, at the narrowest, and that is the figure we run. Ten Spartans with knives can wall off 50 feet. John catches a spear shaft one-handed and snaps it, since a Spartan-II's reaction time is estimated at 20 milliseconds, fast enough that they see things happen in slow motion. He throws a man 30 feet. In canon, Adriana-111, a Spartan in Mark IV armor, lifted a Mongoose rated at 2,370 pounds by the handlebars.

Spartans do get tired, just slower than anyone else. Their augmentations raised their limits; they didn't erase them. Six hours in, Will is a half step behind where he was at dawn. Spears glance off him and he stumbles on the bodies underfoot. The Immortals can't hurt him, but they can bury him. Grace slides in on his left, cuts down the two who found a gap, and buys him 30 seconds to breathe.

Linda breaks the formation. The Immortals hold together because their gold-trimmed officers step out to shout orders. She drops them one at a time, mid-word, until the shouting frays and a gap opens that nobody fills. She keeps one sniper round. By dusk the Immortals pull back. We score it A because everyone is standing, but the armor is scarred, the guns are empty and chapter 1's margin is gone.

Night Assault

A

The Spartans10 Spartan-IIs vs Persian torch columns, fire arrows and the goat-path raiders

Xerxes bets that the Spartans can't shoot what they can't see. But MJOLNIR helmets carry thermal sensors, so every torchbearer glows like a flare. Grace has no bullets left, but she has a dead archer's bow and a pass carpeted with the arrows that bounced off her that morning. Torchbearers drop and the Persian line goes dark in patches. Linda uses her saved round on a cloaked rider well behind the line, comfortably inside the SRS99 sniper rifle's effective range of over 2,000 meters. Night air runs about 25°F cooler than noon, roughly 5% denser, so the round drops more, and a sea breeze pushes it sideways. She holds a hair left and a hair high, and the general falls from his saddle.

Then the Persians stop throwing men at the armor and start throwing physics. Herodotus has Persian archers shooting flaming arrows at the Athenian Acropolis weeks after Thermopylae. No single arrow matters, but in Halo canon a shield only regenerates once the wearer is out of fire, and in Halo: Combat Evolved it waits five full seconds after the last hit. Burning oil stuck to the plate never lets those five seconds begin, so the shields bleed down and can't recover. The flames also run so hot that they wash out the thermal picture.

A line of 30 or 40 men creeps along a goat path, the Anopaea track that a local named Ephialtes sold to Xerxes in the real battle. If they reach the back of the line, the squad is pinned. Kelly runs the slope flat out on loose stone, past her own record. In canon, John once hit about 65 mph in a Mark V training exercise and tore his Achilles tendon. Kelly plants hard on a ledge above a 300-foot drop and something in her left leg tears. She still kills the raiders, and the last one goes off the cliff.

A spear driven into the seam at her thigh skids. Under the plates is an armored bodysuit, and a hand-thrust spear has nowhere near the energy to punch through it. The flank is gone. By dawn the fire arrows have stopped, so the shields climb back. We score it A. Kelly is limping and the armor is scorched black, but nobody is down.

The Elephant Charge

A

The Spartans10 Spartan-IIs vs Xerxes' 12 war elephants

This is the one chapter where something can hurt them. Herodotus never puts an elephant at Thermopylae. That comes from the movie 300. The first time we know a Persian king fielded war elephants is Gaugamela, a century and a half later, where Arrian counts 15 of them. A war bull runs about 4 tons, and Asian elephants have been clocked at 15 mph. That is roughly 90,000 joules, like a 2,200-pound car hitting at 30. It's also close to Hunter weight, and in canon a Hunter's shield once broke the ribs of the Spartan Kurt-051.

John's two rockets finally get used. The first drops the lead elephant sideways across the narrows and the second drops the one behind it, so the carcasses plug the pass like a cork. Fred's last two pistol rounds into the third elephant's face don't stop it, and the herd climbs over the dead. Will has no bullets, so he sets a Persian spear's butt against the rock and lets four tons run onto the point. The elephant dies on its feet and topples across his legs. His shield hits zero, his visor flashes red and the comms go quiet.

This is the worst minute of the battle. Fred is swatted into the cliff wall and his shield flares out. Grace hits him at a dead sprint and shoves him clear, and the elephant's foot comes down on her instead. Her shield takes half and her ribs take the rest. In canon it took Brute Shot rounds to kill Grace, so spear points just skid off her plate. Kelly drags her bad leg and opens a mahout from hip to shoulder.

The elephants decide it. Linda uses her last pistol round on the mahout riding the biggest elephant, from 40 yards. He tumbles off, the animal bolts back into the Persian ranks, and the herd stampedes after it. That matches history. At the Metaurus in 207 BCE, Hasdrubal's elephants ran wild and turned on their own side, and Carthaginian drivers carried a chisel and mallet to kill their own animals if they ran. Four Spartans lift the dead elephant off Will, and his status light winks green. We score it A: zero KIA, though two of them need help up the ramp.

What it comes down to

Ten Spartan-IIs hold the pass because nearly everything Persia owns tops out around 100 joules, while their shields and rifles are built for rounds dozens of times harder. The real threats were fire that stops shield recharge, fatigue, and the 4-ton elephants. They beat those with thermal sensors, saved rockets and Linda's last round, and Persia's own panicked herd finished the job. The cost was every bullet they brought. Overall tier A: the pass holds and all ten go home.
