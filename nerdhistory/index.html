How a verdict is made

The greatest fighting forces in history, dropped into battles they never fought. 300 Spartans at the Alamo, a Navy SEAL team inside Modern Warfare, the Roman Empire in A Quiet Place. Real units, real weapons, real tactics.

Each page is written from the episode itself, then every canon and real-world claim on it is searched and read at the source before it goes up. The winners are the video's; the page shows why.
