TMNT vs Predator: Who Wins and Why

Four cold-blooded ninja turtles are hunted by a Predator across Brooklyn and Manhattan, and each round gets a survival tier from F to S.

The Turtles survive, barely, because water kills the cloak and teamwork does the rest.

Every NerdScience fight

Bebop and Rocksteady

The PredatorBebop and Rocksteady vs the Predator

The Predator wins in about four seconds because Rocksteady charges into a hunter he can't see. A drop of condensation from a catwalk splashes off the invisible shoulder and outlines it for half a second. Donnie spots the bend in the air, but nobody listens, and Rocksteady steps two paces forward right under it.

The wrist blades punch straight through his chest. Real rhino hide runs up to 2 inches thick, and it doesn't matter. A blade driven from point-blank range by a 7.5-foot hunter doesn't care about the hide.

Bebop gets his machine gun halfway around before the shoulder-mounted plasma caster fires. It's the same weapon that blew Dillon's arm off in the 1987 film Predator. He drops with a smoking hole in his chest.

The scene sets the scale. This is the 2016-movie version of the pair, dangerous brutes the Turtles could barely contain. Both are finished in four seconds, and the Turtles' teamwork never gets a chance to matter.

The Kill in the Alley

B

The Predator (the Turtles escape alive)The Turtles vs the Predator

The Turtles lose this round because they can't see what they're fighting. The Predator's cloak bends light around his body, so you see the background instead of the hunter. They only get half-second glimpses, from water. In the 1987 film the hunter's cloak malfunctioned in the water of a river, and that same weakness sets up everything that follows.

Leo decides to run. Raph never retreats, but the Predator's mask catches the light and three red dots settle on the front of his shell. That's the plasma caster's targeting lock. Mikey yanks him sideways just as a bolt blows a crater in the concrete where he stood.

They make the storm drain, but shrapnel from that crater tears Raph's arm open, and the blood trail leads the hunter in. He dips two fingers in it and follows. He doesn't shoot them in the back because the 1987 hunter also toyed with prey to find out what it was worth. B means they survive, wounded and hunted.

The Sewer Hunt

C

The Predator (the Turtles escape alive)The Turtles vs the Predator, underground

Thermal vision should be the Predator's best tool and here it fails. In the 1987 cartoon, a heat-seeking missile fired at the Turtles loops past them and back at Shredder, because they're cold-blooded. A resting human gives off about 100 watts and holds 98.6°F. A cold-blooded body drifts toward room temperature, and in a tunnel that means cold stone. In the red-to-blue mask, the Turtles show up as gray smudges.

The Predator answers by switching modes. Yautja masks carry vision modes beyond thermal, and here he switches to one that reads the electric field every beating heart throws off, so cold-blooded or not, the Turtles light up. Donnie had already found a faint electromagnetic hum under the alley recording. The hunter has the sensor Donnie needed an hour to build.

With that, the Predator finds the trap in under a minute. It's three Foot Clan explosive charges and one trip wire. He stops two feet short and pulls the detonator wire free with two claws. Raph charges anyway. The Predator throws the smart disc, the weapon that cut Agent Keyes in half in Predator 2, and it tears Raph's sai from his hand. A thigh-mounted net gun then pins him to the wall.

Leo's katana cuts a thin white line across the shoulder plate. It's only a scratch, but it's the first mark on that armor in a long time. The Predator taps two fingers to his chest and nods. That fits the honor code the films keep returning to. In 1987 he spared a woman because she was unarmed, and Predator 2 ends with an elder handing a cop an antique flintlock pistol as a mark of respect. Leo gets a pass, and C reflects that.

The Rooftop Gauntlet

D

The Predator (the Turtles escape alive)The Turtles vs the Predator, over Midtown

Up on the rooftops the Predator has the edge. The hunter has no servos, only legs. The films have these hunters leaping tree to tree, and that strength carries over to the city. Splitting up makes him hesitate, but he kicks Donnie off a water tower and backhands Leo hard enough to turn the world white. In 1987 this species overpowered even Arnold's Dutch hand to hand. The Turtles are bleeding on three roofs within a minute.

Donnie's answer is the Times Square billboard. He tethers his phone into the service panel, owns the control grid, and strobes the screens. A flash can't blind thermal, because thermal lenses are germanium, which is opaque to visible light. The strobe works because of the sewer discovery: the mask is tracking electricity. A heartbeat's magnetic field is only about 50 to 100 picoteslas, trillionths of a tesla, and Earth's field is hundreds of thousands of times stronger.

The mask can ignore a field that holds still, and it can ignore one pulsing at heartbeat speed. But Times Square sits in a district that Con Edison estimates draws at least 161 megawatts at any given time, and Donnie flips a whole tower's screens on and off several times a second. To a mask tuned for heartbeats, that's a sun going off. The hunter swings his head looking for a picture that isn't there, and Mikey cracks his mask from behind with the nunchaku.

The mask comes off and green blood runs from the Predator's brow. That's first blood. He drives the combi-stick at Mikey, and Raph takes the thrust through the shoulder. That's a hole, not a death sentence, as long as it misses the big artery under the collarbone. The Turtles escape because the targeting lock lives in the mask: no mask, no cannon. The Predator puts his mask back on rather than finish the chase. D reflects that they can outsmart him but not outrun him.

The Final Stand

A

The TurtlesThe Turtles vs the Predator, in the flooded pit

Donnie builds the trap around the drop of condensation from the alley. In the 1987 film, water disrupted the Predator's cloak. Donnie splices a cable off the site's generator and runs live wire into the shallows of a flooded pit, 15 feet deep and the size of a football field. There's no wire to spot, because the trap is the water. Leo and Mikey bang pipes together on a plank as bait, and the hunter drops in. Sparks crawl up his legs and the cloak dies. It stays dead as long as he's standing in water.

Then Donnie kills the generator. In fresh water, a body conducts better than the water around it, so the current would go through the Turtles too. As little as 10 milliamps, a 50th of what a 60-watt bulb draws, can lock up muscles. The cloak only needed to die once, and now all four can hit him together. Donnie jams his bo staff into the shoulder cannon so the shot hits water, and Mikey hauls a gauntlet down.

Leo meets the combi-stick blade to blade. A sword isn't fan service here: in Predators, a Yakuza swordsman fought one of these hunters in a katana duel and both died of their wounds. Leo pays a smaller price when a wrist blade rakes his plastron and drops him to one knee. Then Raph rises with the last sai. A real sai is dull-tipped and built to parry, so it needs a gap. He finds the seam under the ribs where the armor plates meet and drives it in.

The Predator arms his self-destruct. In Predator 2, the cop Harrigan stops that countdown by cutting off the arm with the hunter's own disc. Leo is on one knee with a cracked plastron and doesn't have that shot. The films can't agree on the blast size, so we go with what's on screen: Dutch outran the 1987 bomb on foot, and so can they. Leo hears Splinter say to protect his brothers, hauls Raph up, and the Turtles run for the Hudson.

The river doesn't stop the blast wave, because distance does that. It stops the heat and flying steel. The Turtles curl up under the surface with their shells out and Leo wrapped around Raph. When they surface, the site is a smoking crater and the Predator is gone. All four are alive, and that earns the A. On raw power the Predator wins every round, but they scratched his armor, cracked his mask and killed his cloak.

What it comes down to

The Predator is stronger in every round, and the Turtles survive by finding what his tech can't handle. Cold blood blanks thermal vision. A strobe overloads an electromagnetic mask. Water kills the cloak, and Raph's sai goes into the one gap in the armor. They don't win on strength. They survive because four prey animals work together, outlast a hunter used to killing alone, and leave the Predator a respect he rarely hands out twice.
