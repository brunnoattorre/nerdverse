Ben 10 vs Justice League: Who Wins and Why

Brainiac runs all seven Justice League heroes on one shared mind, and Ben Tennyson has to survive them using only Omnitrix aliens and a timing flaw.

Ben survives by outtiming Brainiac's network, not by outfighting the League.

Every NerdScience fight

The Street-Level Gauntlet

A

Ben Tennyson (Flash knocked out, Batman crystallized)Batman and The Flash vs XLR8, Four Arms and Diamondhead

Ben starts with XLR8 because speed problems get speed answers, and that's the wrong call. XLR8 manages roughly 500 mph. The Flash here is the Justice League cartoon version, who circles the planet in seconds in the Justice League Unlimited episode Divided We Fall. Three full-weight punches land before Ben sees the first one coming. Brainiac isn't running these two on non-lethal, so nothing is pulled.

Batman then drops from a ceiling vent and clips Ben with an EMP batarang. The Omnitrix hates that pulse. The dial jams for two seconds, which is plenty of time for Flash to turn around. Ben forces it through to Four Arms and survives a hit that would cave in a human rib cage.

Ben stays alive because the speedster isn't the one driving. Wally's reflexes come from the Speed Force, but the network picks every move and sends it down the wire. Every second and a half it resyncs, and Ben sees a half-second green flicker behind the eyes. Flash is moving at a machine's tick rate, so he is only ever where the math says he'll be.

Diamondhead uses that. Ben puts both hands on the floor, waits for the flicker, and grows a crystal wall into Flash's already-computed path. Flash hits it at roughly 700 mph. That works out to about 4 megajoules, around a kilogram of TNT, and he goes down and stays down. Batman gets the gentler version: crystal grown up through the floor, locking him in to the neck with no shard near the skull. Ben isn't going to start killing heroes tonight.

The tier is A, one step below the top. The risk is that if Brainiac ever releases Wally from the leash, the fight ends in one frame.

Escalating Force

A

Ben Tennyson (both heroes frozen)Hawkgirl and Green Lantern vs Fasttrack, Rath and Big Chill

Fasttrack is the bet on evasive speed, and it fails against reflexes good enough to meet it. Hawkgirl's are. Her mace catches him across the chest and sends him through a support pillar. Meanwhile Green Lantern's constructs aren't the usual predictable shapes. Walls thicken where Ben tries to break through, and blades curve toward a dodge before he commits to it. Brainiac is running the ring like a firewall and patching each exploit as Ben finds it.

Rath can take the mace swing on his forearm. The Rath form has taken point-blank laser fire without flinching, so the arm holds. The metal is the problem. Hawkgirl's mace is Nth metal, and it carries an electrical charge, which numbs Rath's whole arm to the fingertips. Standing still to trade blows buys Ben three seconds of watching, and he uses them to notice the flicker getting longer when the two heroes move together. With two bodies in sync, Brainiac's processing splits thin.

Big Chill goes intangible and drifts through a rising construct wall like smoke through a chain-link fence. Ben's plan has one hole. In the Justice League Unlimited episode Ancient History, Gentleman Ghost gets caught in an Nth metal net, and Green Lantern says the metal has properties that interfere with dimensional shifting. Our read is that Big Chill's phasing isn't dimensional shifting, but canon never tested the metal on his species. Hawkgirl tests it, and the mace connects. Two ribs crack.

Ben still wins the exchange by picking the one target the mace isn't guarding. Big Chill goes straight through Green Lantern's chest and leaves the cold behind. A power ring needs a focused mind to hold a construct, and a body frozen to the core doesn't have one, so the wall folds. Hawkgirl comes around for another pass and takes ice breath across both wings. She drops, frozen to the shoulders.

Rath doesn't heal mid-fight, so the ribs stay broken, and that carries into the next fight. The tier is A for three forms burned and one hit taken by trusting intangibility against the one metal that doesn't respect it.

The Mind and the Muscle

Ben Tennyson (Wonder Woman bound, Martian Manhunter dropped)Wonder Woman and Martian Manhunter vs Four Arms, Ghostfreak and Heatblast

Wonder Woman lifts Four Arms like luggage and throws him through a concrete wall, and that's no stretch for the animated Wonder Woman. At the same time Martian Manhunter is inside Ben's head, feeding him three Wonder Women from three directions when there's only one. Ben swings at a shadow, the real one connects, and Four Arms folds. Then the Omnitrix times out at the worst possible moment.

Ghostfreak is the next pick, and the obvious plan, going intangible to hide, isn't what decides it. Ghostfreak's species disrupts electronics by phasing through them. Brainiac's grip on these heroes is nanotech, the same kind the cartoon already put inside Lex Luthor. When Ben drifts through J'onn's body, the nanotech hiccups. Three Wonder Women flicker down to one and the green pulse skips a beat. The control is hardware, and the mind under it is still J'onn's.

That matters because J'onn has a famous weakness, fire. The comics have framed it as Martian biology, as pyrophobia, and as a psychic block placed on Martians by the Guardians of the Universe. Either way it lives in J'onn, and Brainiac is standing on that mind rather than replacing it. Ben quick-changes to Heatblast and throws a wall of flame across the room. J'onn seizes, and for two full seconds the network loses him completely.

The flicker runs through every hijacked body, and Wonder Woman freezes for one beat. Ben quick-changes to XLR8, covers 20 meters in under a tenth of a second, and rips the lasso off her hip. He loops her wrists to a steel beam and is clear before the beat ends. The lasso doesn't care how strong she is, so she strains against her own weapon.

Ben ends the round leaning on a broken pillar, with the timeout, the concrete floor and the cracked ribs behind him. He's alive only because the watch came back before the fist did.

Superman and the Core Network

S

Ben Tennyson (network shattered, Ben knocked out)Superman and Brainiac's Network vs Way Big and Feedback

Superman is the primary vessel, with a full solar charge and no restraint parameters. The animated Superman is weaker than the comics version, but weaker is relative. Ben goes Way Big, 100 feet tall or more, and for four seconds it looks winnable. Way Big once threw Vilgax into orbit, and his return swing puts Superman through the far wall and the wall behind it. Then Brainiac reads Way Big's anatomy.

The frill on Way Big's head is his greatest weakness, and hitting it causes great pain and stuns him, which is documented for the species. Superman comes back through his own hole at three times the speed of sound and puts heat vision into the frill. Way Big drops to one knee, stunned, as the building folds in on him. Brute force has run out.

Ben then remembers what he counted at 2 a.m., the pulse every second and a half, and realizes the seven heroes are one network split across seven skulls. He goes Feedback, a small target that draws ranged fire. Heat vision is an energy beam, and energy is what Feedback feeds on. Brainiac adapts and sends Superman in to finish it by hand. That's the mistake, because contact is what Feedback needs.

At Superman's chest, Ben feels the network riding under the skin. Feedback drains power by touch, so he pulls, waits for the resync window when every node is listening, and shoves everything back down the wire at point-blank range. The network fractures across all seven bodies at once. The punch already thrown was launched by Brainiac, but by the time it lands it belongs to Clark Kent.

That punch is a pulled one, because Clark is a man holding back, and it still puts Ben through a wall and out cold. We rate it tier S because the network breaks before the hit finishes him.

What it comes down to

Ben never beats the League straight up. Every win comes from the same flaw: Brainiac's network resyncs about every second and a half and leaves a half-second blind spot. Diamondhead walls Flash in that gap. Big Chill splits two bodies the network can't run cleanly. Ghostfreak's electronics disruption and Heatblast's fire break J'onn's control. Feedback pushes the network's own power back through it. Each alien works because Ben picks the form that matches the weakness he finds, not because it's the strongest. The cost is real: broken ribs, a timeout and a final punch. If Wally or Superman were fighting for themselves, Ben wouldn't wake up.
