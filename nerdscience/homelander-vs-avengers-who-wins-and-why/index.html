Homelander vs Avengers: Who Wins and Why

We drop Homelander and the Seven into Midtown Manhattan against the Avengers and grade every Supe on the F to S survival scale across four chapters.

Homelander survives at tier B only because Thor spared him.

Every NerdScience fight

Midtown contact

A

The Avengers hold the street; Homelander is untouchedHomelander and the Seven vs Iron Man, Captain America, Black Widow and Hawkeye

Homelander launches at the nearest glowing red and gold target, closing on Iron Man past Mach 1, about 760 mph. Iron Man lands a repulsor blast square on his chest and it barely stings. That tracks: Vought brags in the show that every weapon on Earth has been tried on him and failed. His laugh lasts about three seconds, because the arc reactor is now the brightest target on the block and his eyes are starting to glow.

Queen Maeve grabs a city bus to use as a weapon, and that decides her fight. In the comics she flies. In the show she can't, so once she leaps she is committed. Captain America doesn't block her, he turns her: the shield catches her forearm, he hip-rolls under, and her own momentum drives her into the bus. She stops an armored truck with her body in episode one, so it barely hurts her, but Cap ends up standing over her.

A-Train never sees the steel cable net Black Widow and Hawkeye string between two lamp posts. Call his speed 200 mph, since Fifth Avenue has corners. Stopping that in the 10 feet the net stretches is about 130 G, roughly 11 tons of force, and his shoulder takes the worst of it. The trap targets his exact flaw: the show's plot starts when he can't stop in time and runs through Robin at the curb.

The rest go down in one move each. Black Noir cuts an Iron Man drone in half, and his point-blank arrow miss on Hawkeye is a close call. Starlight drains the block's traffic lights for one flare that frees Maeve. Translucent uses the flash to circle Black Widow, but her Widow's Bite drops him, as a cable did in the pilot, because his carbon skin conducts. Cap's shield and ram floor The Deep, who is weakest away from the ocean. Tiers: Homelander A, Noir B, Maeve and Starlight C, A-Train and Translucent D, The Deep F.

The Hulk problem

A

Hulk dismantles the Seven; Homelander watches from aboveThe Seven vs Hulk, Thor, Black Widow and Hawkeye

A-Train, one arm useless, runs at Hulk anyway, circles him in a red blur and lands a dozen punches. Then he commits to one running hit at about 200 mph. A 170-lb man at that speed carries about the energy of a 2-ton SUV at 40 mph. Hulk doesn't need to hit back. He opens his hand, closes his fingers around A-Train's torso, and all that energy goes into A-Train's own ribs. Hulk then swings him into a parked taxi and the legs shatter.

That crushed rib cage sits on a heart already running on borrowed time. A-Train abused Compound V to stay the fastest man alive, and the show gives him a heart attack by the end of season one. It is why the damage is fatal later, not just painful now.

Black Noir drops onto Hulk's back and his blade barely breaks the skin on the forearm. In The Avengers (2012), Banner says he put a bullet in his mouth and the Hulk spat it out, so a sword isn't going to do it. Hulk grabs Noir by the scruff and throws him through a pharmacy window. Translucent creeps toward Hawkeye on a loading dock, and the arrow clangs off his bulletproof skin. The clang gives him away, and Black Widow's electrified baton finishes it.

Maeve and Starlight try a leap and a face full of light, but Hulk swats them like mosquitoes while tearing a bus in half. Thor then comes out of the clouds, catches Maeve mid-leap, and one short swing of Mjolnir sends her through a window four floors up. Homelander stays an A because he hovers and watches, which fits a man who, in the show, abandons a plane full of passengers after wrecking its controls rather than try to save them. Maeve D, Starlight C, Noir hurt but mobile, A-Train, Translucent and The Deep F.

Homelander goes off

A

Thor wins the exchange that mattered; Homelander flattens Iron ManHomelander vs Thor, Iron Man and Captain America

Homelander goes for the loudest Avenger. Thor is on the Chrysler Building spire, calling down lightning. Beam and bolt meet and Homelander holds off lightning with his eyes for one second. That is his best moment, because Thor doesn't trade beams, he closes. Mjolnir hits his jaw, sends him through a glass skyscraper, and his fingers come away red. In the show, his blood is almost unheard of: Maeve does it in the season three finale and his son Ryan in season five.

Bloodied, he looks down at the phones and goes looking for someone who can't hit back that hard. He dives at Mach 1.5, a number from the show, where city cameras clock him at Mach 1.53, about 1,165 mph. A 200-lb man at that speed carries roughly 12 million joules, about 6.5 lb of TNT, all of it on Iron Man. The suit fails system by system and he ends up in a building, dragging a trench through brick.

Cap plants himself in front of the wreckage and raises the shield. Why isn't that suicide? Vibranium. In The Avengers (2012), Mjolnir hit that shield hard enough to level a forest for hundreds of yards and the shield didn't care. When Maeve leaps for Cap's back as Homelander comes around again, Cap angles instead of blocking. The impact rings like a church bell. Cap skids back 30 feet and Homelander glances off at over 1,000 mph into Maeve's knee, which folds the wrong way.

Starlight drains every billboard on the block and puts it all into one blast between Thor's shoulder blades, which buys Homelander three seconds. Then Thor's lightning lashes out. On paper this is her best matchup, but the show never sets a ceiling on what she can hold, and what we see her absorb is ambient lights and wiring. In The Avengers (2012), one Thor bolt pushed Iron Man's suit to 400% capacity. We call it for the overload: she hits the pavement smoking. Homelander holds A, since he lost the only exchange that mattered and Thor never went to full power. Maeve F, Noir and Starlight D, the rest F.

The last stand

B

Hulk and Thor; Homelander survives only because Thor lets goHomelander vs Hulk and Thor above Times Square

Homelander strikes first. His fist hits Hulk flush on the jaw at Mach 1 and Hulk slides back a full block, about 260 feet, heels carving trenches in the asphalt. It's his best punch of the day, and Hulk grins. He grabs a fistful of cape, yanks him out of the air, and throws the punch that stopped a Chitauri Leviathan in The Avengers (2012). It lands in Homelander's ribs and something cracks.

Why doesn't a man who flies at Mach 1.5 just leave? Because every phone on Seventh Avenue is pointed at him. What Homelander fears most was never a weapon. It's a video: one passenger on Flight 37 filmed him abandoning that plane, and the clip hangs over him from season two until it finally leaks in season five. Flying away on camera is the one movie he can't make. So he stays and goes to the eyes, the same heat vision that took out the mayor's jet in season one. Hulk's skin blisters, but Hulk keeps walking, grabs his leg, and swings him through two buildings and into the street.

Hulk winds up like a discus thrower and hurls him straight up, and Thor is waiting at the top of the arc. He catches Homelander by the collar with lightning crawling up both arms. Thor's first movie, Thor (2011), is about being banished for arrogance and coming back humbled, and he is looking at who he used to be. He decides this one isn't worth killing and opens his hand. A man falling belly down tops out near 120 mph, the gentlest hit Homelander takes all day. Thor knows it won't kill him. That was never the point.

Homelander lies in a cracked street, ribs grinding, teeth full of blood, while everyone films. Nothing in the city could hold him, so the Avengers turn their backs and go help the wounded. A-Train dies, his V-damaged heart giving out under the crushed ribs. Hawkeye cuffs a dazed Deep, and Maeve and Translucent are cuffed where they fell. Starlight goes to the medical lab at Avengers Tower on a stretcher. Black Noir slips away unseen. Final tiers: Homelander B, Maeve F, A-Train F (dead), Noir D, and Starlight, Translucent and The Deep F in custody. You don't get an S for a survival someone else signed off on.

What it comes down to

Homelander survives the hour at tier B because Thor opened his hand, not because anything he threw worked. Thor drew blood with one short swing, Hulk walked through his heat vision, and the thing that hurts him most is the footage on a few hundred phones. Only Black Noir got away clean, and the rest of the Seven ended hurt, captured or dead.
