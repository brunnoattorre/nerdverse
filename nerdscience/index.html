How a verdict is made

Superheroes, villains and video game legends, tested against each other and against their own canon. Could Wolverine survive every Superman villain? Could Kratos solo Game of Thrones? We pull the feats from the source material and do the maths.

Each page is written from the episode itself, then every canon and real-world claim on it is searched and read at the source before it goes up. The winners are the video's; the page shows why.
