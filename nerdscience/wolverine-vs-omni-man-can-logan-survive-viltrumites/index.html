Wolverine vs Omni-Man: Can Logan Survive Viltrumites?

We drop Wolverine into the Invincible universe and test whether adamantium and a healing factor can survive the Guardians, Omni-Man, Battle Beast and Anissa.

Logan survives at tier D, but only because the winners walk away first.

Hostile Welcome

B

No one. Logan escapes through the sewers.Wolverine vs the Guardians of the Globe

Red Rush ends up as a stalemate. Logan can't see him move. Three hits land on his jaw, ribs and kidney, and Logan swings at nothing. A normal person needs about a quarter of a second to register movement. Logan is quicker than normal, but to Rush that's an afternoon. Rush can't put Logan down either. Every punch lands on an adamantium-laced skeleton, and the healing factor erases the bruises almost as fast as they form.

Shrinking Rae gets nowhere. She rides in on his collar, going for his eyes and eardrums, which are thin on anybody. Logan's nose finds her and he sets her on the curb. She's a scared rookie, and he doesn't kill rookies. Then Immortal and Black Samson arrive together. Immortal's punch sends Logan through a storefront window. His ribs hold because adamantium doesn't crack, so everything behind the bone takes the hit. Nothing had hit him this hard since the Hulk, his first big fight.

Logan can't win the Immortal exchange. He opens Immortal's forearm to the bone and the cut seals while he watches. Immortal has a healing factor too. He's the guy Omni-Man had to decapitate in episode 1 of the show, and even that didn't stick. Two healing factors trading cuts is a fight nobody wins. So Logan stops fighting Immortal and drives both claws into the back of Black Samson's knee, where the tendons run.

That cut decides the chapter, and it works because of Rush's job. A teammate bleeding onto the asphalt is exactly what a first responder exists for, so Rush scoops Samson up and is gone. Immortal won't leave a screaming crowd to chase a stranger, at least for about five seconds. Logan uses them to rip up a manhole cover and follow the tunnels until the shouting fades.

We score it a B. He was never close to dying, but he never won either. He got out because the Guardians had a teammate to save. The cost is the real story. His bones never needed fixing. What broke was the soft stuff: a bruised lung, torn muscle, organs. In a hospital, multiple major injuries can push a person's resting burn rate well above normal. Logan did that repair in minutes on no food, and every repair spends fuel he isn't replacing.

Omni-Man Tests the Mutant

Logan and Nolan beat the Reanimen, but a laser nearly kills LoganWolverine and Omni-Man vs Dr. Sinclair's Reanimen

Nolan opens with a pulled punch to the stomach because he wants to know what this stranger can take. In this what-if, the episode 1 massacre of the original Guardians never happened. Nolan broke with the Empire quietly, which is why Red Rush was alive to hit Logan. Logan's claws rake Nolan's forearm open to the elbow. That landed because Nolan wasn't dodging. He was testing whether adamantium can cut a Viltrumite.

Canon never settled it. The two universes touched in Marvel Team-Up #14, where Mark Grayson calls Wolverine 'Claw Man', but they never fight. We go with the Marvel line that adamantium cuts almost anything, and Viltrumites aren't invulnerable. Their delicately balanced inner ear is a known weak spot. Then Nolan slams Logan into an AC unit. Logan's claws shriek across the steel and Nolan's head twitches once, as if someone slid a needle into his ear. Logan files that away.

The truce comes from Nolan's explanation. The last thing to step out of a portal in this city was the Flaxons, an invading alien army, so a stranger from a portal looked like round two. Nolan doesn't trust Logan, but a guy who can bleed a Viltrumite is worth having around. Then four Reanimen land, corpses Sinclair rebuilt into cyborg soldiers. They don't breathe, sweat or flinch, and they want Logan because a body that won't stay dead is the best raw material Sinclair has seen.

Pain-proof enemies don't scare Logan, because he's been killing them for over a century. In the show, three Reanimen nearly killed Mark. Adamantium doesn't need a while. The first goes down in three pieces and the second loses its head before it clears its weapons. The third fires a laser rifle into Logan's chest, which fits the later comics, where Reanimen carry laser firearms. Nolan rips the shooter in half, as he does in the show, and the last one retreats.

The burn is the whole chapter. A clean cut knits shut. A burn doesn't. Tissue proteins start cooking at temperatures that beam blew far past. The cooked layer seals into a hard crust called eschar, and real healing can't start until it comes off. So Logan's body has to tear its own wound open, clear the dead tissue, rebuild, and only then close it. Real burn patients can burn far above their resting metabolism, and he pays that on an empty tank. He lived and gained an ally, but that burn came closer to ending him than anything Chicago threw at him.

Battle Beast Wants a Fight

D

Battle Beast, who walks away and spares LoganWolverine vs Battle Beast

Nolan can't go to the wrecked research outpost in the Alaskan interior, 40 miles from the nearest road. Going would tip off the Viltrumite watching Earth. So Logan goes alone, still stiff from the chest wound. Battle Beast is waiting. He stands 7 and a half feet and weighs nearly 800 pounds. In the show he complains that he was promised worthy opponents here. One punch drives Logan down through three feet of ice into black water. Logan's forearm bones hold, but everything wrapped around them doesn't.

Logan climbs out and gets one real hit in, both claws across Battle Beast's chest. Battle Beast grins at his own blood and starts hitting harder. Each blow lands heavier: ribs, jaw, a knee that folds Logan in half. Every time Logan gets up, the healing costs more, and the clock from Chicago ticks louder. That's the pattern breaking. Getting up stops being enough.

The finishing blow is a fist to the middle of Logan's back, and it shows what adamantium can't cover. The vertebrae hold, but the spinal cord inside is still nerve tissue, and the blow bruises it. Doctors see this in real patients: a spinal cord injury with no fracture on the scan. Nerves are the slow part of healing, and a damaged one in a normal human regrows slowly, if at all. Logan's factor does it in minutes, but minutes is a long time to lie on the ice.

Battle Beast turns to leave because he won't take an uneven fight. In the Invincible comics, when Thragg, the Viltrumite ruler, got gutted mid-fight by a Rognarr, Battle Beast cut open his own stomach to keep it fair. Logan gets up anyway, legs half numb, claws scraping out. Battle Beast could block the last lunge and doesn't, because he wants to know what's left. The claws open a shallow red line across his neck, and he laughs.

In the show he left Mark alive because Mark didn't impress him. He leaves Logan alive for the opposite reason. He wants a rematch once Logan has healed, and he walks off into the white. We score it a D, because Logan is alive only because the thing that beat him chose not to finish the job.

One Viltrumite, One Mistake

Anissa wins. Logan survives, and she leaves with a ruined ear.Wolverine vs Anissa

Anissa has landed with orders to kill Nolan for turning on the Empire. She's the Viltrumite who was watching Earth, and she's billed as faster than Nolan himself. Nolan has been fighting for days without rest, so if she catches him tired, Earth loses its best defender. Logan picks the ground. He calls her on her own radio channel and tells her the traitor is waiting at a collapsed steel mill, a maze of beams and shadows. She arrives expecting Nolan, and hits Logan before he finishes turning, sending him through a support column.

The speed gap decides most of the fight. Lowball her at 10 times the speed of sound, and be generous to Logan with reflexes twice a normal person's, about an eighth of a second. In that time she covers more than a quarter mile. He can't dodge or block that for a full minute. He just stays alive by rolling behind girders and healing on credit.

Then her arrogance shows, the flaw Viltrumites share. In the show, Nolan spends a whole fight lecturing his own son between punches. Anissa grabs Logan by the throat and holds him at arm's length for a good look at the thing that won't stay down. That leaves no distance for her speed to matter. Logan remembers the rooftop and Nolan's twitch. Viltrumites fly using a delicately balanced inner ear, and in canon the right sound can drop one in agony. The comics revealed it in Invincible #111, and the show has the government mounting noise makers on a building wall.

Logan has no noise maker, but he has claws. Doctors see a tiny version of this from cotton swabs pushed too deep, which can injure the balance organ, and the vertigo can floor a grown adult. Logan drives one claw into her left ear. She drops him, grabs her head, tries to lift off and corkscrews sideways into a girder. For about five seconds a Viltrumite can't tell which way is up, and Logan is winning.

It doesn't last, and that's our call. Canon never says whether adamantium gets through a Viltrumite skull or how long an inner-ear hit keeps one down. It does say she's faster than Nolan and that Viltrumites heal fast, and dizzy isn't slow. Her backhand catches him mid-lunge, and then she hits him faster than he can rebuild. The healing factor doesn't quit, it falls behind. Her one mistake is not checking the body. She flies off crooked toward Nolan, and 20 minutes later Logan radios a warning: she's lost the ear, she can't fly straight, she heals fast, so move now.

What it comes down to

Logan's healing factor works like a credit line, and every chapter spends more of it: a rib-crushing punch, a burn eschar, a bruised spinal cord, and finally a beating he can't rebuild fast enough. The Invincible universe's fast, strong fighters decide whether he gets to touch them. Anissa let him close once, and it cost her an ear, but she still won. We score the whole trip a D. He survives at the top of this world only because the winner walked away first, and what he leaves behind is the one weakness Viltrumites don't expect anyone to use.
