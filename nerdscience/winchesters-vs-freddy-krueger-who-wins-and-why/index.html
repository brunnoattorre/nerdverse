Winchesters vs Freddy Krueger: Who Wins and Why

Sam and Dean Winchester take a case on Elm Street to test whether a hunter's kit can touch a killer who exists only in sleep.

The Winchesters win, but only by dragging Freddy out of the dream.

Every NerdScience fight

Bodies on Elm Street

B

Freddy KruegerSam and Dean Winchester vs Freddy Krueger

Four teenagers are dead in their own beds, each with four parallel cuts at the same spacing and no forced door or window. The glove is the one fixed fact of the franchise. It is a custom-made razor glove built in the boiler room where he worked, and it stayed the constant while his origin was rewritten across the series. That is the detail to scale from.

Their witness, Tommy, has been awake 72 hours. CDC figures put 24 hours without sleep at about a 0.10% blood alcohol level, above the 0.08% legal limit, so he is already past drunk. He nods off mid-sentence and four cuts tear across his chest. Dean's EMF meter shows nothing and a salt line along the table does nothing. Both tools are built for ghosts, and Freddy is not one.

At the boiler room, the cold spot is just a draft off standing water. A blade shrieks down a pipe, the steel door slams, and the brothers run to the car. The door was never shut. Dean was asleep on his feet for about 9 seconds, a microsleep, which typically lasts a few seconds and can run to about 30, and which you never notice. The EMF and the salt were right. Dean walked into Freddy's world.

Sam names the closest precedent, the Season 3 Supernatural episode "Dream a Little Dream of Me", where African dream root put hunters inside Bobby's dream. Dean points out that case had a living man with a tea habit, someone he could stab. Freddy is dead and wears a hat. The B is for hunters who were prepared and sharp but whose whole trunk could not register him once.

Dean's nightmare

D

Freddy KruegerDean Winchester vs Freddy in the dream

Dean has been awake 36 hours when the coffee cup tips in his hand and he wakes in a motel room that is wrong. The shotgun and the iron knife are gone, because every answer he owns stayed in the waking world. Freddy opens with Dean's file: 40 years in Hell. In Supernatural that was four months on Earth, 30 years on Alastair's rack, then 10 years holding the knife himself. Freddy always attacks with the victim's own history.

Dean does the hunter thing and goes forward. He catches the glove arm at the wrist and drives Freddy into a pipe hard enough to shear it. For about two seconds he is winning. Then the hallway lengthens, the pipe unshears, and the wall he just hit is behind him. Freddy's power is authorship, not strength. He rewrites the floor plan faster than Dean can cross it, and the glove opens Dean's ribs. A red line splits Dean's shirt on the motel mattress.

Slaps and an ice bucket do nothing because Dean is stuck in REM paralysis. In the dreaming stage the brain paralyzes the muscles so you do not act out what you see, and Freddy holds Dean inside it. Dream Warriors (1987) shows the same trap, where Joey is left in a coma while the adults stand around his bed doing what Sam is doing now.

Sam's last resort is the jumper cables, clamped to the bed frame and Dean's belt buckle. Twelve volts cannot push meaningful current through dry skin, but a car battery can drive hundreds of amps through a dead short and heat metal fast. It is a brand, not a shock, and pain still gets through paralysis. The buckle goes orange and Dean comes up screaming. He lands the only clean hit of the night, and it buys two seconds. That is a D for Dean, and a B for Sam's rescue.

Race to the boiler room

Freddy Krueger, for nowThe Winchesters vs Freddy's clock and the bone burn

Tommy fell asleep around five, and Freddy took the last kid in under two hours, so the clock is short. Dean drives with a bandaged arm, the lane lines drift, and the road goes black. Three blades punch through the dash and Freddy's face bulges out of the windshield. Sam grabs the wheel and hauls the Impala off the guard rail. It was a two-second microsleep. At 60 mph the car covers 88 feet a second, so 176 feet were driven by nobody. Sam jabs a pocketknife into Dean's palm, because pain keeps you awake.

At the Springwood Library microfilm reader they find the history. Fred Krueger was arrested for killing around twenty children and freed because a search warrant was signed in the wrong place. The parents of Elm Street tracked him to the boiler room where he worked and burned him alive. There was no grave and no burial record. Sam calls it a vengeful spirit, so the Winchester answer is salt and burn.

Dean knows the brothers' own rulebook says otherwise. You salt and burn the body, but if the body was already burned, the spirit is tied to an object instead. By that rule, the bones are the wrong target before anyone picks up a shovel. Dream Warriors (1987) also ran the test. Freddy's remains at Penny Brothers Auto Salvage were laid in consecrated ground with holy water and a crucifix. Freddy possessed his own skeleton, killed a man with it, and the series went on with him.

So the brothers switch to the only thing that has ever worked: pulling him out. Dream root plus a piece of Tommy's hair as his DNA drops Dean into Tommy's dream, where Freddy will be. As they pack, a blade rips through the wallpaper and Sam throws the motel's iron poker through the wall, then wakes mid-throw with 11 seconds missing. The real drywall shows no howl, no blood and no scorch. It is the kind of trick Freddy runs on his victims, blurring dream and waking. Iron drops ghosts in Supernatural and did nothing here.

Burn him out

A

Sam and Dean WinchesterSam and Dean Winchester vs Freddy Krueger at Penny Brothers Auto Salvage

Dean drinks the dream root tea in three swallows and stands in the boiler room, where Tommy is backed against a furnace with the glove four feet from his throat. Dean grabs the glove arm and this time it holds, because he knows he is dreaming. In Supernatural, someone who knows they are dreaming can take control, as Bobby does in "Dream a Little Dream of Me", and a walker who knows where he is can bend the place. Dean bends a pipe into a crowbar and swings it into Freddy's jaw. Then he turns to Tommy and tells him he is dreaming and can wake up. Tommy blinks out.

Outside, Sam opens the rusted trunk and finds charred bone and black soil in a rotted shoe. He lights the salt and lighter fluid and the bones stand up, as the skeleton did in Dream Warriors, and Sam's shovel swing gets his arm opened. Inside the dream, Freddy ignores the fire and puts Dean back on the rack, 30 years at once. Lucidity runs on knowing which room you are in, so Dean's control collapses and the glove pins his shoulder to a boiler.

Dean's hand closes on the Impala key in his coat pocket. The key was planted in the first minute. Pain was the anchor in the diner and on the highway, and the key biting his palm stops the room from sliding. He gets both hands on the glove arm and wakes up holding on. This is the one move across the series that has taken Freddy out of play: Nancy in 1984, Maggie in 1991 and Lori Campbell in 2003. Out here Freddy is flesh, breathing hard on wet gravel.

Sam drives the iron poker through his chest and pins him. It works not because iron hurts ghosts but because it is three feet of steel and the man is meat. Dean takes the shotgun and fires rock salt from 10 feet, close enough to hurt a man badly. Lighter fluid and the burning remains finish it. Sam gets an A. Dean gets a C, lower than it looks, because the bone burn failed and only handed Sam a skeleton to fight.

What it comes down to

The Winchesters win because Freddy's only real defense is the dream, and the one thing he cannot stop is someone gripping him on the way out of it. Everything they brought on instinct, the EMF meter, the salt and the bone burn, was aimed at a ghost, and it failed. Pain as an anchor, dream root and the wake-up grab worked because they treated Freddy as a man with a power. Once he was in the waking world, he was flesh. The films have never made a Freddy death stick.
