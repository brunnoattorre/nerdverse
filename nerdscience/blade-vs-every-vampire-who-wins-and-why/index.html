Blade vs Every Vampire: Who Wins and Why

Blade has five serum vials, one teak stake and an hour to sunrise while four of fiction's deadliest vampires hunt him in a locked warehouse.

Blade survives all four, barely, with no serum left and the sun's help.

Every NerdScience fight

Morbius

A

BladeBlade vs Morbius

Morbius wins the opening seconds because he hunts by sound. His treatment left him with bat traits, partly hollow bones that let him glide and echolocation that maps a dark room through screams. When the lights die, Blade can't see him, and Morbius opens his forearm and sends him into a stack of crates.

The fight turns on one tool. Blade drops a flashbang at his own feet and shuts his eyes. A military flashbang peaks around 170 to 180 dB within five feet. Morbius has been filling the room with his own shrieks, so now every surface answers at once and his sonar picture turns to noise. He staggers into the open, blind and clawing at his ears.

Blade's silver rounds and UV flash barely matter. Morbius is a lab accident, not a supernatural vampire, so silver hits him like it would hit a person, and bright light pains him without burning him. What settles it is skill. Morbius has all the power and none of the technique. Blade steps inside a swing and puts the sword through his heart.

The heart is the one wound Morbius can't heal. The comics give him a healing factor that handles bullet wounds but can't regenerate lost limbs or organs, and a heart split by a sword is lost. Blade wins, but Morbius drew blood, and the spilled blood wakes his thirst. He uses a vial, which leaves four. That is why the tier is A.

Lady Dimitrescu

B

Lady Dimitrescu (Blade escapes)Blade vs Lady Dimitrescu (Resident Evil Village)

Lady Dimitrescu is a size problem first. Capcom lists her at 9 feet 6 inches, and her claws are long enough to cut through a tapestry and the oak paneling behind it. In Resident Evil Village those claws took Ethan's hand clean off. She grabs Blade by the throat with one hand, lifts him off the floor and drives him through a banquet table. His ribs crack.

Blade can't trade blows with her, so he fights the building. He runs into a narrow servants' corridor built for people half her height. Her shoulders scrape both walls and those long arms have no room to swing. He drops low and cuts both tendons at the back of her ankles. She goes down on her knees.

That doesn't matter, because she heals in seconds. Her cuts close before Blade can follow up. The game backs this up: bullets only stagger her. The one thing that ever shut off her healing was a poison dagger hidden in her own family crypt, and even then she didn't go down for good. She turned into a huge winged, dragon-like beast and kept hunting Ethan.

Blade carries nothing like that poison, and nothing else in his kit stops a body that rebuilds itself. So he does what players do in that castle and runs. He's through the far door before her ankles knit. He walks out hurt and thirsty and takes a second vial early. Three are left. Tier B: he didn't beat her, and nothing in his kit could.

Alucard

C

No winner: Alucard spares BladeBlade vs Alucard (Hellsing)

Alucard can't be killed by anything Blade carries. The sword takes his arm off at the elbow and then cuts him in half at the waist. His shadow flows over the pieces, stitches them together, and he stands up with an unmarked red coat. Those faces in the shadow are the souls he has drunk. The Hellsing manga's final chapter counts 3,424,867 of them.

His guns do the damage. The Jackal weighs about 35 pounds, is about 15 inches long and fires 13 mm explosive rounds, roughly half an inch across. The Casull fires custom explosive 454 rounds cored with a melted silver cross. Silver means nothing to a Daywalker, but the blast throws Blade into a wall. Then Baskerville, the black hound, tears at his legs.

There is no clever trick here, because Hellsing doesn't give Blade one. Alucard survives lethal wounds by spending the souls stored inside him, and he comes back every time. This is also only his restrained form, since full release needs his master's permission. A sword through the throat gets a grin. A full magazine into his skull knits shut before the casings land.

What ends the fight is a bite. Alucard pins Blade and breaks the skin of his neck. In Hellsing, drinking someone's blood gives Alucard their memories, so he gets Blade's whole life: 20 years of needles and a thirst he never gave in to. Alucard's creed is that a monster should fall to a human. He lets go, steps back and walks off. Blade survives because Alucard chose to let him. Tier C.

Dracula

C

BladeBlade vs Dracula (Marvel, Tomb of Dracula)

This is Marvel's Dracula from Tomb of Dracula, the 1970s series where Blade debuted. Blade has hunted him ever since. Dracula is far stronger than Blade. He catches the sword with one bare hand and snaps the titanium. Then he throws Blade across the room.

The rest is powers Blade can't match. Dracula turns to mist, then to bats that hit from three directions, and the broken sword cuts only shapes that dissolve. His eyes find Blade's and a voice in his head says to put it down. Blade drives the teak stake through his own palm. The pain tears the fog away and the voice breaks.

The ending comes from Dracula's two rules in the comics. Direct sunlight is deadly to him, but it isn't instant, and he can turn to mist to get away. A stake through the heart holds him still. Blade needs both. The stake holds him still and the sun finishes him.

Blade backs under a cracked skylight while Dracula follows, in no hurry. He smashes his last vial into Dracula's face. Serum in the eyes buys half a second, enough to rip the stake from his hand and drive it through the heart. The first sunbeam comes through the cracked pane and Dracula blackens and flakes away. Blade holds him there while the thirst claws at his throat. The same light does nothing to a Daywalker. Tier C: he won only because the sun showed up on time.

What it comes down to

Blade gets through all four, barely: two kills, one escape, one act of mercy and not one vial of serum left. Morbius falls to a flashbang and a sword through the heart. Lady Dimitrescu and Alucard can't be put down, so he survives by running and by earning Alucard's respect. Dracula falls to a stake and the sunrise. Every wound tonight worsens his thirst and every dose of serum works a little less, so the sun and his Daywalker blood are what decide the night.
