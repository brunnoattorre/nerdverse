Avengers vs The Last of Us: Who Survives and Why

Five Avengers face ten years of The Last of Us, scored F to S at Day 1, Year 1, Year 5 and Year 10.

The Avengers survive ten years, tier B, but lose Clint and Bruce.

Every NerdScience fight

Day 1: Runners in Boston and Hawkeye's bite

A

The Avengers win the street, but Clint is bittenAvengers vs Runners

Raw power is not the problem on Day 1. Steve drives his shield into a runner and sends it 20 feet into a parked car. Hulk plows through a pack of them. Thor's bolt takes out about 200 infected, and Clint picks off runners with arrows because arrows are silent and noise pulls in the next dozen. Steve's evacuation likely saved hundreds of people, which is why the team gets an A.

The deciding weakness is that none of their tools scare this enemy. Steve's fighting style is built on making people quit, and the runner just gets back up. Thor's thunder means nothing to infected who don't fear thunder or death, so a second wave walks around the corner right after the smoke clears. Friday also flags the fungal signature as natural, so there is no villain with a plan to outsmart.

The bite settles the day. A runner lunges at Steve's back in a collapsing building, and Clint shoves him clear and takes the bite through his sleeve. In the game, anyone bitten turns within two days at most, so a bite is an F. The real Ophiocordyceps takes 4 to 10 days to work on an ant, and researchers found it never touches the brain. It wraps the muscles and runs the body like a puppet. Clint gets a D only because Tony is already pulling a gauntlet apart to build him a sleeve.

Year 1: the quarantine zone and the first Clickers

B

The Avengers hold the Boston breach but lose the zone's trustAvengers vs Clickers and Fedra

Clint is still alive a year after the bite, which is more than 180 times what the game allows. That is possible because Tony built a sleeve that pumps a suppressant through the arm. It slows the fungus and doesn't stop it, and this is our most generous call, since nothing in the game has ever slowed a bite. It fits Tony's history, though. In Iron Man 2 his own reactor was poisoning him and he watched his blood toxicity climb. Now Friday reads out Clint's number every morning.

The east gate falls to a few hundred infected, and the first Clickers are among them. They are blind and hunt by echo like a bat. Thor and Hulk hit the breach hard, but every roar and thunderclap pulls in more Clickers from blocks away. They win by going quiet. Thor throws one bolt into the gap and stops. Hulk shoves an armored Fedra truck into the hole and holds it there without a sound.

Fedra's rule is that anyone infected gets executed, and Steve refuses to give up Clint. Clint then lunges at him, fast but with no technique left. Steve won't raise his shield against a friend, so he takes a hit to the ribs. He still catches Clint's wrist, turns him into the wall, and holds him while Tony sedates him. Fedra gets Clint in a locked cell, and the team's trust is gone.

The B comes from a split verdict. Nobody in the zone can match the team, and they held a breach against an enemy that hunts by sound. But the suits are running down with no power grid, and in Iron Man 3 Tony had to plug a dead suit into a charger in a Tennessee garage. Thor is eating half of what he needs. He could call the Bifrost home but won't leave the team, and no help is coming from Asgard.

Year 5: the wasteland, infected Hulk and Ellie

C

Thor and Mjolnir pin Hulk, but the fungus wins the teamThor vs infected Hulk

Boston has fallen and the team looks like survivors. Tony's reactor puts out maybe a fifth of the three gigajoules per second he bragged about in the first Iron Man. He walks now and rations his repulsor shots. Then a flooded subway tunnel hits them with spore-heavy air, the game's signature threat, which the HBO show cut from season 1. Steve and Thor get their masks on. Hulk doesn't know a mask exists, and by morning gray plates are pushing up on his shoulders.

We call the result a stalemate. A fungus eats its host, and Hulk's tissue is a buffet that refills itself. His healing factor and the fungus lock up, and he can't turn back. Thor: Ragnarok showed Hulk staying out for two years straight, and it left Bruce afraid he might not come back next time. That is why the strongest Avenger falls to the fungus first.

In the stadium, Thor's first bolt hits Hulk square in the chest. In Ragnarok a bolt like that sent Hulk flying through the air. Here it doesn't matter, because the thing driving the body doesn't care what it costs. Hulk rakes Thor open and pounds him into the turf. Thor's answer is Mjolnir. Its enchantment only checks whether you are worthy, as the Avengers party scene in Age of Ultron shows, and a fungus doesn't make anyone worthy. Thor slams the hammer between Hulk's shoulder blades and lets go, and Hulk is pinned.

Steel beams shouldn't hold a Hulk, and in the cage it is Mjolnir across his chest that does it. The rest of the time it is whatever is left of Bruce, choosing to stay. They then find Ellie with a three-week-old bite, and Tony's scan shows the fungus doing nothing in her. Friday says one word: immune. We pull Ellie forward on purpose, since in the game she isn't born until years after Outbreak Day. The team is hardened but splintering and short on supplies, so the tier is C. Hulk is now a threat to everyone near him, so he gets an F.

Year 10: last stand at the Firefly lab

B

The Avengers reach the cure, and Hulk falls holding the lineAvengers vs Bloaters, Clickers and Stalkers

It takes five more years and thousands of miles to reach the Firefly lab under a ruined hospital in Salt Lake City, with Hulk's cage on a flatbed. Steve's shield has lost its paint, but vibranium doesn't crack, and it took Thanos to break it. The stakes are real, and a fungal vaccine would be extremely rare. Bloaters finally show up too, since the game says they need several years of fungus.

The tools run dry in the lab. Tony drops the lead bloater with his last repulsor shot, then his suit locks up and Thor has to tear the chest plate off by hand. Thor pulls every bit of lightning the sky has left and clears nearly the whole first wave. A second wave is already pouring in, and Thor is gasping. The team has force left, but no reserves.

Then comes the choice from the game. The Fireflies' notes say the cure means cutting the fungus out of Ellie's brain, which kills her, and Joel answers that with a rifle. Steve's answer: we don't trade lives. So Tony takes a needle sample instead of the whole growth. Neil Druckmann, the game's co-creator, has said the Fireflies' cure would have worked, and nobody ever tested a sample. It takes hours, and Steve has to buy them.

Steve buys the time by unlocking Hulk's cage and saying the two words that always worked: Hulk smash. Hulk and Thor fight back to back through hundreds of infected, and Hulk pulls apart bloater after bloater. Then he drops, not from the bites but because the stalemate and this fight burn his last reserve. The needle works, and Tony broadcasts the formula over the Fireflies' radio. Clint was never found and Bruce never came back, so the final tier is B with a heavy asterisk.

What it comes down to

The Avengers beat an alien army in a single battle, but the fungus took ten years and cost them more than the Chitauri ever did. Strength was never the deciding factor. Every win came from a tool that fit the infection, like silence against Clickers, Mjolnir's worthiness test against infected Hulk, or a needle sample instead of a scalpel. Every loss came from the bite, the spores or the empty power grid. They survive because Steve refuses each trade the world offers: Clint for the walls, Ellie for the cure, Thor for the time. Joel chose Ellie over the cure, and Steve tries to get both.
